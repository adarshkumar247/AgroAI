Action Plan & Guidance

bolt What You Can Do (Immediate Care)
First 24 Hours
1
Prune affected lower leaves

Carefully snip symptomatic leaflets using sanitized shears; seal and dispose strictly outside field boundaries to halt fungal inoculation.

2
Adjust Irrigation Method

Switch from overhead sprinkler to targeted drip irrigation lines to keep upper tomato foliage dry and suppress sporulation.

3
Mulching Ground Layer

Apply clean straw or UV-stabilized plastic mulch around plant bases to prevent soil-splash spore transmission during rain or watering.
