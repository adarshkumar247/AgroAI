## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intelligence, precision, and tranquility of modern precision agriculture. Crafted to evoke quiet authority, immediate trust, and high operational clarity, the visual language merges the systematic elegance of Material 3 with the natural vitality of botanical science.

The target audience spans agricultural enterprise operators, agronomists, greenhouse managers, and field technicians who demand instant diagnostic clarity under direct sunlight as well as in low-light command stations. The experience rejects aggressive high-tech tropes in favor of an organic, calm, and dependable interface. 

The aesthetic philosophy is **Botanical Functionalism**:
- **Clarity over ornament:** Interfaces prioritize agronomic signal (soil metrics, weather anomalies, crop vigor) over decorative clutter.
- **Organic geometry:** Generous, organic corner radii paired with structured, grid-disciplined data architecture.
- **Tactile feedback:** Soft elevation, airy touch targets designed for field usability, and delicate botanical tints that reduce cognitive fatigue during prolonged analysis.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-order computational workflows in natural earth tones, balanced by surgical diagnostic accents.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B5E20`):** Forest canopy green. Used for key action states, brand anchors, primary navigation headers, and authoritative confirmations.
- **Secondary (`#81C784`):** Fresh botanical sage. Used for secondary active indicators, progress milestones, selected states, and organic graph accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0288D1`):** Steel irrigation blue. Reserved for meteorological data, soil moisture telemetry, AI analytical prompts, and cold-chain monitoring.
- **Neutral (`#1A1C19`):** Deep organic charcoal. The primary high-contrast reading ink, replacing stark digital black to reduce eye strain while retaining outdoor legibility.

### Surface Tiers & Containers
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAF8`):** An ultra-soft, warm natural white canvas reflecting ambient sunlight without harsh glare.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority operational cards, active dialogs, and detached sheets.
- **Surface Tint / Container (`#F1F8F4`):** Soft mint neutral used for secondary data grouping, inactive chips, and module backings.
- **Surface Accent Container (`#E8F5E9`):** Light forest tint used for active metric containers and confirmed AI action summaries.
- **Subtle Stroke (`#E0E5DD`):** Organic hairline boundary used for delineating cards and list rows without visual heaviness.

### Agronomic Diagnostics & Status Tokens
- **Vigorous / Optimal (`#2E7D32`):** Peak crop health, normal operational parameters.
- **Action Required / Moisture Stress (`#E65100`):** Severe pest pressure, drought threshold, equipment lockouts.
- **Elevated Warning (`#FBC02D`):** Moderate microclimate variance, nutrient imbalance caution.
- **Critical Failure (`#D32F2F`):** Frost alert, sensor offline, toxic threshold breached.
- **Informational / Atmospheric (`#0288D1`):** Cloud cover forecasts, systemic recommendations.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic roles. Its clean geometry, open counters, and subtle friendly curvature deliver the contemporary clarity of Google’s modern product suite while ensuring rapid scanning across sunlight-saturated tablets and high-density desktop dashboards.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used exclusively for high-level macro telemetry (e.g., total field acreage, overall harvest yield forecasts) and hero metrics. Kept tight in letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) to maintain density.
- **Titles:** Set in medium and semi-bold weights for card headers, module summaries, and inspection checkpoints.
- **Body Text:** Engineered with generous vertical rhythm (`1.5x` minimum) to preserve reading comfort when scanning deep AI diagnosis briefs, soil health reports, and agronomic playbooks.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Compact and slightly tracked out for badges, metric unit signatures (`kg/ha`, `pH`, `m³/h`), and pill chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive 8-point spatial grid paired with a flexible fluid column system. It provides high structure for complex field telemetry while maintaining breathable whitespace to reduce cognitive fatigue.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column responsive layout with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates persistent left-rail navigation, multi-field satellite map panels, and deep data sidebars.
- **Tablet (640px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` (24px) and `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px). Supports flexible 2-up module stacking for field diagnostics.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px). Single-column stack prioritized for rapid single-handed field input.

### Spacing Rhythms
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled visual pairs (metric icon to numerical value, input field label to field stroke).
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, chip collection spacing, and control bar alignment.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for separation between distinct thematic metric modules within the same view.
- Use `space-xl` (32px) for structural division between major workspace regions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tonal layering**, refined translucent blurs, and soft ambient light shadows, strictly rejecting harsh artificial drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Floor):** `#F8FAF8` flat canvas. Zero elevation. Host to spatial grids, maps, and backdrops.
2. **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Low Modules):** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted mint (`#F1F8F4`) surfaces paired with a crisp structural hairline border (`1px solid #E0E5DD`). No blur shadows required; structure is purely tonal.
3. **Level 2 (Standard Telemetry Cards & Interactive Modules):** `#FFFFFF` cards resting with a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -4px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(26, 28, 25, 0.03)` with a hairline boundary (`#E0E5DD`).
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Hover, Floating Action Buttons & Quick Menus):** Elevated state yielding `0 10px 24px -6px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(26, 28, 25, 0.04)`.
5. **Level 4 (Modal Sheets, Diagnostic Drawers & AI Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an agricultural blur overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(248, 250, 248, 0.82)`), accompanied by deep soft shadowing: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 28, 25, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** (Level 2) shape archetype, invoking modern Material 3 conventions that convey softness, organic harmony, and safety.

### Geometric Scale
- **Micro Radii (`rounded-sm` / 4px):** Selection indicators, progress bar ends, small tooltips.
- **Button & Control Radii (`rounded` / 8px):** Form fields, standard text inputs, action buttons, table row selections.
- **Interactive Pill Radii (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Metric summary modules, actionable notification banners, interactive tags.
- **Card & Sheet Radii (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Master diagnostic cards, crop anomaly inspectors, map overlays, floating sheet drawers.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Filter chips, state badges, AI query prompt fields, and avatar badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1B5E20` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-lg`), height: 44px (field accessibility compliant), radius: 12px. No drop shadow in default state; subtle ambient tint on hover.
- **Secondary Action:** Filled with soft mint container `#F1F8F4`, text in `#1B5E20`. Hover transitions smoothly to `#E8F5E9`.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Transparent fill, 1px hairline border `#E0E5DD`, text `#1A1C19`. Hover triggers `#F1F8F4` surface fill.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter & Selection Chips:** Height: 32px, `rounded-full` shape.
- **Inactive:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E0E5DD`, text `#43483E`.
- **Selected:** Tinted container `#E8F5E9`, border `#81C784`, text `#1B5E20` accompanied by a micro leading checkmark icon (16px).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Minimal 48px container, radius: 12px. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `#E0E5DD`. Active focus transitions to a 2px outline in `#1B5E20` with zero blur glow.
- **Helper Labels:** High-contrast neutral `#1A1C19` positioned above the field; contextual helper/error messages in `#5B6356` or `#D32F2F`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded geometry (radii: 4px for checkbox; circular for radios). Inactive states use `#5B6356` borders; active states flood with `#1B5E20` containing pure white glyph marks.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Field Metric Cards:** Encased in `#FFFFFF` with 20px or 24px corners and subtle `#E0E5DD` hairline boundary. 
- **Internal Padding:** `space-md` (16px) on mobile, `space-lg` (24px) on desktop.
- **Header Stack:** Displays a 20px botanical outline icon within a circular `#F1F8F4` badge, paired with an uppercase muted metric label (`label-md`).

### AgroAI Diagnostic Copilot Module
- **Conversational & AI Prompt Bar:** Floating stadium pill (`rounded-full`) with a crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, soft Level 3 ambient shadow, and a subtle glowing trailing button in `#1B5E20` containing a sparkle/leaf micro-icon.
- **Confidence Badges:** Mini pill tags displaying AI confidence ratings (e.g., "98% Confidence • Nitrogen Deficiency") using tinted semantic backgrounds (`#E8F5E9` background with `#2E7D32` bold label text).
